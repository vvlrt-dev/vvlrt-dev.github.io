FAQ

Questions before your first order

When should I drink it?

During or right after training that lasts longer than an hour, or any session where you finish with salt on your skin. On easy days, plain water is usually enough.

Why 500 mg of sodium?

Sweat typically carries several hundred milligrams of sodium per liter, and heavy sweaters lose more. 500 mg per 500 ml sits in that range without tasting like seawater.

How does the subscription work?

You get the same pack every 30 days at 15% off. You can change the flavor, skip a month or cancel from your account before the next ship date.

What if I don't like the flavor?

Write to us within 30 days of delivery and we'll swap it for another flavor, even if the pack is open.
