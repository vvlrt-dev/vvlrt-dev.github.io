Our kazan

One pot sets the rhythm of the day.

Plov takes about four hours from cold kazan to first plate. We start before sunrise so the rice is ready for the lunch crowd, and we cook one batch a day. When the kazan is empty, plov is off the menu until tomorrow.

  1. 07:30

    Fire and oil

    Cotton-seed oil heats in the kazan until it smokes. Onions and lamb go in first.

  2. 08:45

    Zirvak

    Hand-cut yellow carrots, cumin and chickpeas simmer into the base.

  3. 10:15

    Rice and steam

    Soaked devzira rice is layered on top, then the kazan is covered to steam.

  4. 12:00

    First plate

    Served with quail egg, kazy and a fresh achichuk salad.
