FAQ

Common questions

Who counts as a staff member?

Anyone you put on the rota in a billing month. Managers who only build schedules are free.

Can I run several locations?

Yes. Each location has its own rota and coverage rules. Staff who work at two sites count once.

What happens when I go over 10 staff on Starter?

Nothing breaks. You'll be asked to pick Team or Business before you publish the next rota.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans cancel at the end of the month. Yearly plans are refunded for unused full months.
